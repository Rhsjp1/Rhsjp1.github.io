You don't need to kill your lawn. You need to outcompete it.
Sheet mulching with 6-8 inches of wood chips over cardboard smothers turf in 8-12 weeks without herbicides. The lawn becomes soil food, not landfill waste.
📈
3 / 7
The roadmap phases your transition by season — not by weekend.
Fall: site prep and sheet mulch. Winter: root decomposition. Spring: native plug installation. Summer: establishment irrigation. Rush it and the turf rebounds.
🎯
4 / 7
Not every lawn should become meadow. Soil type decides.
Clay-heavy soils favor sedge meadows. Sandy sites thrive with warm-season grass polycultures. The roadmap matches plant communities to your actual soil conditions — not Pinterest.
📊
5 / 7
Phase 1 is the only phase most people complete. Here's why.
Sheet mulching kills turf but leaves bare wood chips. Without Phase 2 (native plug installation at 18-inch centers), invasive weeds colonize within one growing season.
