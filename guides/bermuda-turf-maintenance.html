Bermuda Turf Maintenance Summary

Mowing heights, fertilization timing, and dormancy management — the single-page reference every Bermuda owner needs.

Key Reference Points

Mowing Height by Season

Spring: 1.5" | Summer: 2" | Fall: 1.75" — never remove more than 1/3 of blade height in a single cut.

Fertilization Windows

Green-up (April), peak growth (June), pre-dormancy (August). Skip fall nitrogen — it invites winter kill.

Dormancy Triggers

Soil temp below 55°F for 5 consecutive days. Stop watering, stop mowing, let it sleep.

Winter Overseeding

Perennial ryegrass at 5-10 lbs/1000 sq ft in October for green color without harming spring green-up.
